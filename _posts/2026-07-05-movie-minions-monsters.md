Score: 2/5 🤷️

> This is the rambunctious, ridiculous and totally true story of how the Minions conquered Hollywood, became movie stars, lost everything, unleashed monsters onto the world and then banded together to try and save the planet from the mayhem they had just created.
>
> — [TheTVDB.com](https://www.thetvdb.com/movies/minions-3)

While some parts of the movie were quite funny and enjoyable, I didn't really find the movie that good overall. The story didn't feel as if it flowed smoothly. The decision to split in two parallel happenings didn't work well for me. Especially as both were progressing quite slowly until they joined up again.

Also, because of some scenes, this movie didn't feel as kid-friendly as the previous ones. It is not that they were very problematic but just unnecessary considering Minions have been primarily designed for kids till now.

If you are going to watch it, you will have fun. But if you miss it, I don't think you will be missing much. Just watch the two long minion-mayhem scenes later online.
